bookeh colour themes, round 2

All five keep the Nordic slate greys and add peach. They differ in the bright colour and where it goes. The fills are gone: the lent chip is an outline, the open book is marked by a bar at the left edge with no tint, the Rows / Covers switch is an underline, and the Scan button is an outline. Only number 5 keeps one fill, on the Scan button, so you can compare.

1. Peach only

Peach is the only colour, used as lines: button outline, selection bar, underlines, the lent chip. No bright.
