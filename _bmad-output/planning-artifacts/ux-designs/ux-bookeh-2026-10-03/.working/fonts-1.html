bookeh typefaces

The type-led direction rests on its typeface, so here is the same screen in seven. Each row shows the collection in light mode, a book's details, and the collection in dark mode. All of these are free to use and can be served from your own server. This page loads them from Google Fonts, so it needs an internet connection to show them correctly.

T1. System fonts

Whatever the device ships: San Francisco on Apple, Segoe UI on Windows, Roboto on Android. Nothing to download, but the app looks different on each device.
