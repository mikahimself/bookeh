bookeh colour themes

Five palettes, each in light and dark. Colour only: the font, the layout and the shapes are placeholders. The cover colours are the same in every theme, so you can judge each palette as a frame for real covers. The highlighted row is the book that is open in the panel, and the filled chip is a lent copy.
