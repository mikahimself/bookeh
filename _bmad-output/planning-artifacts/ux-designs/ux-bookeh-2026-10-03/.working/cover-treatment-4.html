Locked (Mika, 2026-10-26): light gray, accents 1 in 5, white 1 in 5; the bar opens on these. DESIGN.md → Colors has the values.

Round 4, revised (2026-10-26). Base: the Hävitys blue (#012534, sampled from Iida Rauma's cover), a pacific cyan (#3B8EA5) in place of the Severance terminal, which sat too close to the Hävitys blue, a near-black charcoal (#1C1C1C), a gray after Saana Airtola's Musta aalto (the light candidate leads; none was sampled), and the warm white of the Tuntematon sotilas stand-in, now about 1 in 6. Accents: the mixtape yellow at full strength and its pink slightly faded.

A hash of the title fixes each book's colour, so it never changes with sorting or filtering; the price is that two whites can still land side by side now and then. "Rare" white keeps that unlikely; "never adjacent" picks again when the tile before it is white, which looks right in every view but means a book's colour can change when the order does.
