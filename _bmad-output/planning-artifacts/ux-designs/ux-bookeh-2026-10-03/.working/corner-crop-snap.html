Corner crop: snap and side handles

Indication A (heavier accent edge) at 1° tolerance, as you chose. New here: a gentle snap to vertical and horizontal while you drag a corner, and a diamond at the middle of each side that moves the whole side. Drag a corner or a diamond, or focus one and use the arrow keys (1 photo pixel, Shift 10). Arrow keys never snap. On desktop, hold Alt while dragging to skip the snap.

Snapping judges each side the corner touches, so a corner can land exactly on both a vertical and a horizontal at once. A side moved by its diamond keeps its angle, so there is nothing for it to snap; the two sides that meet it change angle and are marked as usual.
