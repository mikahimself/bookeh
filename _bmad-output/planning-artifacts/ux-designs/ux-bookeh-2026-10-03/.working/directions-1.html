bookeh design directions: how much Metro

Three directions for the collection screen. All three use a white background, the slate greys, coral as lines only, and no fills. They differ in how much they borrow from Metro. Each shows the desktop in light mode with rows and a book open in the panel, and the phone in dark mode with covers. The navigation labels are placeholders; the menu structure is not decided yet.

First: which coral

Four lighter corals, each on white and on the dark background. The directions below use K2.

A. Type-led

The strongest Metro borrowing. Large, light, lowercase headings do the work that boxes and lines usually do. The section names sit side by side as text, with the current one in full ink. There are no dividers between rows, and no outlines except on buttons. Corners are square.

B. Grid-led

Metro's structure without its big type. Small uppercase labels, square corners, firm two-pixel outlines on controls and hairlines between rows. Covers sit close together like a wall of tiles. It is the densest of the three.

C. Soft slate

The lightest Metro touch, and the closest to what you picked in the last round. Hairlines, slightly rounded corners, pill-shaped chips and ordinary headings. Only the white background and the coral have changed.
