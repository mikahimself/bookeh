bookeh wireframes: density and book detail

Layout and behaviour only. The greys, the font and the borders are placeholders; colour and type are decided separately. Everything here is clickable: the density and width switches work, and tapping a row opens the book.

1. Density in the collection view

Three ways to give you three levels. Each list is cut to the same height, so what you see is what fits on one screen. Try each at phone width too.

One layout. The levels change the cover size, the row height and how many lines of text each book gets.
