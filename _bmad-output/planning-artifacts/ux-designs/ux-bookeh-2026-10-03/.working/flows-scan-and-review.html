bookeh flows: after a scan, and the review screen

Phone screens in the type-led style, light mode. These are static pictures of each step, read left to right. The wording on the screens is a placeholder; we settle the voice later. "Find by title or author" lives on the scan screen, as you chose.

S1. Always the answer first

Every scan lands on the answer. Adding with details is one of the choices there.

  • Shop check: the answer appears with no taps, whether or not you have the book.
  • Cataloguing: one tap per book if the fetched data is fine (add to library), two if you want the review screen (add with details, save).

S2. A switch on the scanner

The scanner has two modes. It stays in the mode you last chose.

  • Shop check: the answer appears with no taps, provided the switch is on check. If you left it on add, you land on the review screen instead.
  • Cataloguing: one tap per book (save), after setting the switch once.

S3. Depends on the book

No switch. The app decides from whether you already have the book.

  • Shop check, a book you have: the answer appears with no taps.
  • Shop check, a book you don't have: you land on the review screen, with "not in library" as its heading. Wishlisting takes two taps (pick wishlist, save).
  • Cataloguing: one tap per book (save).
