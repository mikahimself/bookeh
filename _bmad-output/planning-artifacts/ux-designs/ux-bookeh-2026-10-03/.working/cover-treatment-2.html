Chosen (2026-10-26): layout C; mixtape palette with the Lumon blue in place of the MDR teal; full strength in dark mode too; Trebuchet 600 caps; Perlin mottle; inset hairline; Finnish hyphenation; the title on the cover at s too. The bar opens on these; the other options stay for the record.

Option C from the first pass, refined: every tile has the title and author underneath, and a placeholder cover carries the title only, in caps, set like the Leviathan wakes stand-in. Settled in this pass: Perlin mottle, inset hairline frame, no hinge. Light mode uses the palette at full strength. Dark mode darkens the same colour, so a book keeps its colour in both themes: "deep" lowers the lightness and keeps most of the saturation; "tint" mixes it into the dark ground, which greys it (yellow turns olive, pink turns mauve: the earthy look again); "full" is the light-mode colour unchanged.

Round 3, less earthy. Palettes drawn from your references: the blue of Iida Rauma's Hävitys, the pink of Pontus Purokuru's Mikä internetiä vaivaa, the green of Seven endless forests, Plur1bus black and yellow, Severance, Amélie. The hex values are from memory, not sampled; hover a swatch for its source. The title takes dark or light ink per colour, whichever reads better (hot pink and yellow get black caps), except Plur1bus black, which gets yellow, and the Severance terminal, which gets terminal cyan.

Long Finnish compounds are the hard case for caps: POSTITOIMISTO no longer fits a column at l. "Long words" compares breaking anywhere (today's break-words), hyphenating with lang="fi" (words of 9+ letters only, at least 4 each side), stepping the size down by the longest word's length, and both. Here every title hyphenates as Finnish; the app uses each Book's stored language, Finnish when it has none.

Each book gets one of the palette's four colours from a hash of its id (the title here), so the shelf is mixed but stable. A book never changes colour between visits, and the noise pattern is seeded from the same hash. Two neighbours can land on the same colour; with four colours that happens about one time in four.
