Corner crop: straight edges

Each frame is live. Drag a handle (or focus it and use the arrow keys, 1 photo pixel per press, Shift for 10) and bring a side to vertical or horizontal. The stage is always dark, so only dark tokens are used. Handles are 24px; the 44px tap area is unchanged.

Every option says “straight” with something besides colour (UX-DR55): a heavier line, a tick, or a dashed guide.
