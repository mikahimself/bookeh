bookeh examples: cover treatment 3

Round 3, after seeing the shipped colours on a real shelf: too much colour. Here most placeholders take a muted cloth (off-whites and pastels) and only a share of them the bold mixtape colours, which stay as they are. Everything else is as shipped in 4975de7: Trebuchet 600 caps, Perlin mottle, inset frame, hyphenation, the title at s, the same colours in both themes.

The shelf is bigger than before (30 placeholders, 4 photo stand-ins) so the balance shows. Each book's choice is fixed by a hash of its title: one part of the hash decides muted or bold, another picks the colour. The bar changes only the shares and sets; a book never changes colour on its own.
