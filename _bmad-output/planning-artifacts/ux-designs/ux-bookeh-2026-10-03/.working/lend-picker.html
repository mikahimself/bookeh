Lend picker

Story 6.7 [J4]. Lend on an owned copy's line in Book detail opens one overlay holding the Person, the date (today by default) and the confirm: a bottom sheet on the phone, a dialog on wide screens. Three ways to arrange it. The scenario is EXPERIENCE.md's: Antti at the door, already in People, today's date. Strings in English; Finnish follows the same layout.

Antti, todayNew person, todayAnother date

B · Rows, then confirmLend → Antti → Lend (3)Lend → type in New person → Lend+ date field

A · Form: combobox, date, Lend

The Person is the Combobox from EXPERIENCE.md, focused on open with the keyboard up. Typing narrows the People; a name that isn't there shows Add "{name}" as the last option. Under it the Date field (today), then Lend, the one primary button. Scales to any number of People, and is the only layout that reads the AC literally (combobox). It costs typing even for a regular borrower.

B · Rows, then confirm

Like Move: People as rows (the ones lent to most recently first, at most six, then Find a person… opens the combobox in place). A tap marks the row with the 8px marker but does not lend. New person is a field under the rows, as "New location" is on Move. Date field, then Lend. No keyboard for a regular borrower, and you see the name before committing. One more tap than C.

C · Tap a name to lend

The Move pattern as it is: one tap on a name lends, at the date shown at the top. The date line reads "Today · 26.10.2026" with a Change Link that turns it into the Date field. A new person is a field under the rows with a secondary Lend button beside it, as on Move. Fastest at the door (two taps), and the toast's Undo covers a wrong tap. The risk: a mis-tap lends at once, and a date change has to come before the name.
